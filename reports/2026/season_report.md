## Method

Standings, all-play, and luck are calculated from stored final snapshots. Expected wins are `(all_play_wins + 0.5 * all_play_ties) / (league_team_count - 1)`. Luck is `actual_win_equivalent - expected_wins`.

Luck is a schedule-result metric. It is not a claim about managerial skill.

Power rankings and simulation probabilities are analytical. They are not official Sleeper standings, playoff seeds, or final promotion and relegation positions.
